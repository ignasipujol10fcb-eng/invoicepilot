Why use a dedicated tracker?

Spreadsheets and documents can work when you have only a few invoices. A lightweight workflow becomes useful when unpaid invoices start getting buried in other work. InvoicePilot keeps the basic invoice-to-follow-up process together without pretending to replace accounting software.
