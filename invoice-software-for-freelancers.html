What InvoicePilot is — and is not

InvoicePilot is designed around the invoice-to-payment workflow for independent professionals. The starter tool works locally in your browser. It does not currently replace accounting or tax software, and it does not process payments itself. You can add a payment URL from the provider you already use.

Freelancer invoicing questions

Can I try InvoicePilot without an account?

Yes. The starter invoice generator is designed to work without a server account.

Can I use my existing payment provider?

Yes. The invoice generator accepts a payment URL that you already use, such as a hosted payment page.

Does InvoicePilot provide accounting or tax advice?

No. It is an invoicing workflow tool and should be used alongside whatever accounting and tax process is appropriate for your situation.

What is Pro?

Pro is intended for freelancers who want the hosted product and additional workflow capabilities as they are released. The current checkout is manual, so access is activated only after payment is independently verified.
