# InvoicePilot — first-customer sales plan

## Ideal first customer

Start with solo freelancers and small service businesses that send several invoices each month: designers, developers, translators, consultants, tutors, marketers and small agencies.

## Positioning

**InvoicePilot is the lightweight invoicing workflow for freelancers who don't want a full accounting suite.**

Core promise:
- Create a professional invoice quickly.
- Track what is still unpaid.
- Put payment instructions or a payment link on the invoice.
- Prepare a polite follow-up without manually rewriting it.

## Offer for the first 10 customers

Founding-user price: **€9/month** while the product is early. In exchange, ask for honest product feedback. Do not promise features that do not exist yet.

## Outreach message

Subject: simpler way to track unpaid invoices

Hi [Name] — I’m building InvoicePilot, a lightweight invoicing tool for freelancers and small service businesses. It creates professional invoices, tracks unpaid invoices and prepares payment follow-ups without the complexity of a full accounting platform.

I’m looking for a few early users to test it. You can try the free version here:
https://ignasipujol10fcb-eng.github.io/invoicepilot/

If it saves you time, I can set you up with the early Pro plan for €9/month. No pressure — I’m mainly looking for real feedback from people who invoice clients regularly.

## Where to prospect

- Upwork and other freelance marketplaces: target active freelancers with recurring client work.
- Indie Hackers: participate in relevant discussions without unsolicited promotion.
- Freelancer communities and professional groups where self-promotion is allowed.
- Personal networks and warm introductions.
- SEO pages targeting searches such as “simple invoice generator for freelancers”, “invoice payment tracker”, and “invoice reminder tool”.

Do not spam communities. Follow each community's rules and only post promotional material where it is permitted.

## Daily sales loop

1. Find 10 relevant prospects.
2. Check that they are a plausible fit before contacting them.
3. Send a short personalized message where outreach is allowed.
4. Offer the free tool first.
5. Track replies and objections.
6. Improve the product based on repeated objections.
7. Ask satisfied early users for a testimonial only with their permission.

## Current evidence

Recent public discussions show a recurring demand for simple invoicing, payment tracking and professional payment details without the overhead of full accounting suites. Examples include r/smallbusiness and r/Invoice discussions in 2026. Treat these as directional market signals, not proof of demand or sales.
