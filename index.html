Simple answers

Is this accounting software?

No. InvoicePilot is intentionally focused on the invoice-to-payment workflow. It should not be presented as tax or accounting advice.

Where is my starter data stored?

The current starter version stores saved invoices in your browser using local storage. Clearing browser data can remove them.

Does InvoicePilot process payments today?

Not yet. The current tool accepts a payment URL that you already use. Payment processing should be connected only after the business and payment account verification is complete.

Why is Pro more expensive than the free tool?

The paid value should come from hosted data, automation and reliable payment workflows — not from putting a paywall in front of a basic invoice generator.
