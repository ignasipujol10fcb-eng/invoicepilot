# InvoicePilot — customer acquisition plan

## Priority
Get the first 10 real paying Pro customers. Do not optimize for vanity traffic before learning why people pay.

## Zero-spend acquisition
1. Direct one-to-one outreach to relevant freelancers and solo service businesses where contact is permitted.
2. Helpful participation in communities that allow product recommendations; answer the underlying invoicing/payment problem first.
3. Publish useful, honest content around invoice creation, payment reminders, and getting paid professionally.
4. Use focused landing pages for search intent: freelancer invoice, consultant invoice, tutor invoice, web designer invoice.
5. Ask early users for specific feedback and, only with permission, truthful testimonials.

## Funnel
Landing page → free invoice/reminder tool → clear Pro value → payment page → payment claimed → manually verified → 30-day Pro access.

## Metrics to record
- Landing visits
- Free-tool starts
- Invoices created
- Pro checkout visits
- Payment claims
- Verified €19 payments
- Activated customers
- Renewal payments
- Reason for non-purchase

## Rules
- No fabricated customers, revenue, reviews, testimonials, scarcity or conversion rates.
- No unsolicited mass messaging.
- No paid ads without explicit approval.
- Do not publish private banking or wallet information.
- Do not claim automatic recurring billing while the payment method is manual.

## Product principle
InvoicePilot should earn money by being useful, calm, transparent and professional: help the customer create the invoice, make payment clear, see what is outstanding and follow up without awkwardness.
