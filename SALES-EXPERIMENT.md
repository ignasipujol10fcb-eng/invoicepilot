# InvoicePilot — Sales Experiment

## Goal
Work toward €5,000/week in revenue without fabricated results, paid acquisition, deceptive claims, spam, or irreversible financial/legal actions.

## Highest-impact improvement
The current product can create, save, print and track invoices locally, but Pro checkout is not yet a real online checkout. Therefore the immediate bottleneck is conversion infrastructure, not adding more generic invoicing features.

## Current verified result
- Verified paying customers: 0
- Verified revenue: €0
- Do not infer traffic, leads, conversions, or revenue without evidence.

## Funnel to build
1. Qualified visitor lands on a niche page.
2. Visitor creates a useful invoice immediately.
3. Product demonstrates the invoice → payment link → follow-up workflow.
4. Visitor sees a clear Pro/Business offer.
5. Secure merchant-hosted checkout completes payment.
6. Payment status is verified server-side.
7. Customer gets access to paid functionality.

## First verticals
- Freelancers
- Tutors / online teachers

These are starting experiments, not claims of product-market fit.

## Ethical acquisition
Use only legitimate, relevant channels. Prioritize useful educational content, search-intent pages, partnerships, directories/communities where promotion is allowed, and personalized outreach where appropriate. Do not scrape private data, spam, impersonate people, fabricate testimonials, or claim customers that do not exist.

## Revenue strategy
€5,000/week is approximately €21,667/month. A €9/month plan alone would require roughly 2,408 active subscribers to reach €21,667/month before fees, taxes, refunds or churn. Therefore the eventual model should combine Pro volume with higher-value Business/team/agency plans, while keeping pricing tied to genuine additional value.

## Next implementation order
1. Secure merchant-hosted checkout.
2. Server-side payment verification/webhooks.
3. Paid-account entitlement system.
4. Basic funnel event measurement with privacy-conscious analytics.
5. Improve retention: reusable clients, recurring invoices and reminders.
6. Run controlled niche acquisition experiments and keep only channels that show verified conversion.

## Decision rule
If traffic exists but free-tool activation is weak: improve landing page/UX.
If activation is strong but paid conversion is weak: improve offer, trust and checkout.
If conversion works but acquisition is weak: improve the highest-performing organic/partnership channel.
If customers churn: improve recurring value before scaling acquisition.

## Operating principle
Be useful, honest, polite and professional. The objective is real customer value and real revenue, not the appearance of success.
