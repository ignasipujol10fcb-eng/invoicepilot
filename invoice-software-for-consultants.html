Why not just use a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet can be perfectly adequate for a small number of invoices. InvoicePilot is useful when you want the invoice itself, payment link, unpaid status and follow-up step to feel like one focused workflow. It does not claim to replace accounting or tax software.

Questions consultants may have

Does InvoicePilot handle client payments?

No. You can place a payment URL you already use on an invoice, but InvoicePilot does not currently process the client's payment itself.

Is it accounting software?

No. InvoicePilot focuses on the invoice-to-payment workflow and is not tax, legal or accounting advice.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. The current starter tool runs locally in your browser.
